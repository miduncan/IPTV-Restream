# IPTV Restream

Shared language for finding sports games and choosing IPTV channels to watch them.

## Language

**Games tab**:
The sidebar tab alongside Chat and Channels for finding games and their candidate channels. Its initial league scope is NFL and college football.

**Game**:
A sporting contest between teams, with a scheduled start and, when available, a reported live status.

**Candidate channel**:
An IPTV channel that may carry a particular game.

**Confidence score**:
A ranking from 0 to 100 measuring how strongly a candidate channel matches a game. It is not a calibrated probability that the channel carries the game.

**Game-window guide listings**:
The EPG programmes overlapping a game's matching window, as examined when its candidate channels were ranked. A listing may be present without supporting a match.

**Specific channel identity**:
The distinct broadcast service a channel represents, such as ESPN or ESPN2. Picture-quality labels do not change this identity.
